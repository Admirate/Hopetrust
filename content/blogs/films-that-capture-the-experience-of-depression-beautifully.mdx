It is frequently tricky to sleep, work, and appreciate life when suffering from mental health difficulties, especially depression. And the loved ones also suffer in their attempt to cope. Movies about depression can offer a powerful lens for understanding and healing.

People with depression might feel lonely, detached, or unmotivated or suffer from more serious symptoms. (That’s why movies about it are so important.) It is difficult for people outside of the condition (hence why movies about it are important).

Family and friends may be comforted when movie stars discuss ([8 Leading Causes of Mental Health Issues](/blogs/8-leading-causes-of-mental-health-issues/)) or their films reflect them. Although those suffering themselves may identify with a few characters in the movie and seek solutions and methods to cope with their problems, they may also feel connected.

Mental health is a popular and critical issue in art in general. Here are some movies that accurately address it.

## World’s Greatest Dad



People with mental health issues are often not noticed or treated with compassion, even though there is a lot of stigma surrounding mental health. When someone dies from ([Everything You Should Know About Depression](/blogs/everything-you-should-know-about-depression/)), people are often highly sympathetic. Lance Clayton, a high school poetry teacher and aspiring author, has a son who despises him and a girlfriend who wants to keep their relationship secret. After his son’s sudden death (from an autoerotic asphyxiation accident), the father makes it appear like he committed suicide. He even writes an eloquent suicide note, which inspires him to continue writing under his son’s name, although his son is dead.

The character Clayton (portrayed by Robin Williams in a heartrending bit of irony) is the one who is down. His sadness enabled him to write such a convincing suicide letter. People who are suffering in silence but are not recognized are subtly portrayed in this film.

## Sylvia



Sylvia Plath, the creator of The Bell Jar, was a prominent writer. She wrote about her depression and marriage to poet Ted Hughes (Daniel Craig). The downbeat and articulate movie portrays Plath’s (Gwyneth Paltrow) suicide. Hughes’ difficulties living with her are deftly depicted. Despite the controversy surrounding Plath’s death being attributed to his infidelity, he felt guilty about it for the remainder of his life. (https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0325055/) portrays how having an extramarital affair can negatively affect a marriage, how someone with depression lives, and how the condition can negatively impact anyone in its vicinity. Sylvia’s writing is reflective and authentic.

## Cake



Claire suffers from chronic pain that makes her very angry after losing her son in a tragic car accident. Her medication abuse results from her denial that her son has died. Although grief can be painful and distracting, if it lasts for more than six months, it is deemed a depressive disorder.

(https://www.imdb.com/title/tt3442006/) is an image of the harmful repercussions of grief and melancholy and how they can lead to other dangerous symptoms, such as excessive sleeping, a lack of interest in activities, and irritability. It might be helpful for individuals who do not quite understand the seriousness of these conditions. Jennifer Aniston delivered a powerful performance in this gloomy movie.

## Numb



When Hudson Milbank feels detached from his body, mind, and surroundings, he seeks psychiatric help in this film, led by Matthew Perry. He is diagnosed with depersonalization disorder. He is prescribed several medicines, but when they don’t work, he consults with another doctor who thinks alternative medications will work. He attempts to win her love when he meets a beautiful girl and takes her on as many exciting adventures as possible.

Although drugs are frequently necessary for individuals with depression, (https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0795439/) criticizes the tendency of modern medicine to over-prescribe drugs without directly addressing any critical issues and the difficulties that ensue.

## The Skeleton Twins



In one of the best dramatic movies from funny comedians, estranged twins Maggie (Kristen Wiig) and Milo (Bill Hader) reconnect after Milo’s suicide attempt and Maggie’s suicidal ideation. Since they feel like they are at the end of their ropes, Maggie and Milo struggle to discover pleasure and purpose in life, causing them to make irrational and destructive choices. They seek to repair their relationship to cope with their depression. In the process, they discover how to do so. To understand what it’s like to have depression, The Skeleton Twins encourages you to examine your sensations of loneliness and recognize how tricky it is for those around you to deal with your condition.

## Anomalisa



(https://www.imdb.com/title/tt2401878/) is a stop-motion animation film about a customer service professional named Michael Stone who perceives himself as an outcast and feels alienated from the world. Michael Stone has clinical depression and is an expert in customer service. He has difficulty establishing connections with anyone and feels isolated from the world. He sounds nearly identical to all the other characters in the movie (voiced by Tom Noonan) except for two. His depression manifests as a result of his existential difficulties in living. Lisa is an anomaly in his world and stirs something in him. This thought-provoking film delves into the hardships of love and relationships. It is a masterful depression film by an expert writer/director. It explores selfishness and decisions considering mental illness.

## Interiors



Caregivers of those with chronic depression and dementia may find it challenging to maintain their patience and compassion. It is important to emphasize and understand that depression is a serious mental health condition affecting millions of people. This film depicts a family in turmoil after the father leaves the mother because he is too exhausted to care for her, who has chronic depression and dementia.

In the film (https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0077742/), Joey is left to care for her mother, Eve, after her father’s suicide attempt. It is a stark depiction of how damaging it can be to be around loved ones who are depressed. Rather than taking her frustrations out on her mother, Joey takes them out on herself, causing herself extreme bodily harm. It is an excellent example of how extremely tough it is to be patient with someone suffering from depression and how simple it is to abandon a relationship.
