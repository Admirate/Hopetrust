_Every day, we face choices that shape our lives. These decisions are guided by our values, morals, and overall well-being. But what truly motivates us to engage with life each morning? The answer, distinct for each of us, is one essential force: purpose, and therapy can help illuminate what that is for you._

With purpose in mind, let’s explore what this concept truly means and how it influences our lives.

## What is Purpose?

Purpose is the inner force driving you to seek meaning and connection, fuelling action and ambition. While searching for purpose is a universal experience, the journey is deeply personal. It sparks our efforts, making us feel part of something bigger.

## What Purpose is NOT

Purpose is not a final destination or ultimate value. It’s a set of pursuits, shaped by your evolving identity. You build purpose through action and reflection. As you change, so does your purpose, allowing you to set your path and redefine your journey.
