Online counselling and teletherapy have revolutionised mental healthcare and mental health treatment lately. Particularly in India, where stigma and a lack of resources have long been obstacles to seeking treatment, digital therapy platforms have become a game-changer as awareness of mental health issues and a demand for readily available support rise.

A ray of hope, online therapy is showing to be just as successful as conventional in-person sessions for managing anxiety and depression, handling relationship problems, parenting difficulties, addiction recovery, and even psychiatric treatment.

This blog explores how teletherapy is revolutionising mental health treatment in India, its advantages, and the reasons millions of people are choosing it as their first choice, since it provides comfort and relief.

### **The Rise of Online Therapy in India**

India has long suffered a significant disparity in mental health treatment availability. The (https://www.who.int/) (WHO) estimates that although less than 30% of Indians seek treatment due to stigma, cost, and a lack of qualified professionals, almost 15% of the population suffers from mental health disorders.

The adoption of telemedicine—including mental healthcare services—has gained popularity during the COVID-19 epidemic. Online platforms saw users rise as individuals turned to online therapy for support. Teletherapy marks a permanent shift in mental healthcare delivery, not a passing fix.

Access to teletherapy services comes from several platforms, which provide phone, chat, and video calls, among other possibilities. These sites link consumers with licensed experts, thus increasing the availability of mental healthcare compared to past years.

_Accessibility:_ Many Indians—especially those living in rural areas—have limited access to qualified therapists. Online counselling closes this void by linking users with licensed experts via video, chat, or phone.

_Affordability:_ Digital platforms offer flexible pricing, subscription models, and even free helplines, while traditional therapy can be costly.

_Convenience:_ In-person therapy can be challenging to schedule due to busy schedules and long distances. Teletherapy saves time and effort by allowing patients to attend sessions conveniently in their homes.

_Social stigma:_ Many Indians may be reluctant to visit a therapist. Anonymity is also a factor. More people are seeking help since online therapy gives more privacy and discretion.

### **Is Online Therapy Really Effective?**

Studies indicate that teletherapy is equally beneficial for many mental health disorders as in-person treatment. According to a study reported in the (https://openaccesspub.org/journal/psychological-disorders), online cognitive behavioural therapy (CBT) was equally effective in India for treating depression and anxiety, with a success rate that is equal to or even more than in-person counselling. This information emphasises the efficiency of teletherapy in India.
