## For Children and Adolescents

1.  **IQ Test**

An IQ test assesses cognitive abilities, measuring their problem-solving skills, logical reasoning, memory, and comprehension. It helps educators and psychologists tailor appropriate learning strategies and interventions to support academic development.

2.  **Autism Evaluation**

An autism evaluation comprehensively assesses a child’s social communication, behaviour patterns, and sensory sensitivities. It aims to diagnose autism spectrum disorder (ASD) by observing the child’s interactions, communication skills, repetitive behaviours, and interests. This evaluation assists in understanding the child’s unique needs and designing specialized therapies and support programs.

3.  **ADHD Assessment**

An ADHD assessment examines a child’s attention span, impulsivity, and hyperactivity levels. Through behavioral observations, rating scales, and interviews with carers and teachers, it helps diagnose attention-deficit/hyperactivity disorder (ADHD). This evaluation guides the development of strategies to manage symptoms and improve the child’s focus and self-control.

4.  **Depression Screening**

Depression screenings in children assess emotional well-being, mood changes, and behavioral patterns that may indicate depressive symptoms. The screening helps psychologists evaluate the child’s feelings of sadness, hopelessness, changes in appetite or sleep, and loss of interest in activities. It aids in early detection and appropriate interventions to address the child’s emotional health.

5.  **Behavioral Analysis**

Behavioral analysis involves observing and analyzing a child’s actions, reactions, and responses to environmental stimuli. It assesses behavioral patterns, social interactions, and emotional regulation to understand the underlying reasons for certain behaviours. This analysis helps create behaviour modification plans, teach coping strategies, and implement interventions to support the child’s behavioral development.
