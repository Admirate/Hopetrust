It is _hope_.

Research indicates that optimistic people have better mental and physical health over the long term. They also typically lead happier and longer lives. People with high hopes have a different perspective on the world, react differently, and concentrate on their controllable thoughts.

**The secret power of hope**

Our level of hope can greatly impact our lives; people with high hopes have passion and zest. This passion is sustainable and dynamic because it involves a certain amount of perseverance and follow-through. They believe in possibilities and have a positive outlook on the future. Instead of viewing challenges as barriers, they see them as chances to develop and learn. For many people with high hopes, this is the secret to success. They recover from failures and appear to continue moving forward despite the difficulties. They think that if they work hard, opportunities will present themselves.

**Ways you can develop hope**

Concentrating on what you can control is the key. Here is a guide to help you develop the habit of hope:

_Establish and meet objectives_

Setting goals is not just a task. It’s a powerful tool that empowers you to take control of your life. Those who are optimistic understand this and take action to reach their goals. They know it’s easy to get distracted and lose focus without a clear objective. They actively make choices and take action to advance rather than letting life happen to them. The hopeful usually has something in mind, whether a big dream or a modest objective. Typically, they envision their goals and establish favourable circumstances for success. This can be achieved, for example, by setting short, manageable micro-goals. Simple examples include making lunch in the next 20 minutes or sending three emails in the next 15 minutes.

_Be with positive people_

One strategy to maintain an optimistic outlook is to surround yourself with positive people. This is not about avoiding the hardships, negativity, or struggles of others but about finding a supportive and uplifting environment. Optimistic people don’t isolate themselves from pessimistic or naysaying people. Despite the negativity around them, they manage to keep a healthy balance. They know that observing various viewpoints enables them to live a realistic life. They maintain their compassion even as they consider many occasionally challenging points of view. By surrounding yourself with positive people, you can feel supported and uplifted, which can help you maintain an optimistic outlook.

_Pay attention to the here and now_

Hope-filled people often concentrate on the here and now. They set goals for themselves but don’t obsess over the past or fret about the future. They maintain their attention on the present. This enables them to remain optimistic and act. Hopeful people are also lifelong learners. Their ongoing efforts to improve themselves and their skill set promote contentment and a growth-oriented mindset. _Be confident and self-reflective_

Although self-belief is essential, very hopeful people can forgive themselves. This is a unique combination because (https://hopetrustindia.com/blog/how-your-self-esteem-affects-your-life/) can be flawed. They are also prepared to take calculated chances to foster their development. People who are hopeful understand that failure is unavoidable and that it’s critical to grow from the experience to move forward with momentum. This ability to reflect on oneself is beneficial. Seeing failure as an opportunity rather than a gauge of one’s value is essential to learning from mistakes. It’s not about avoiding failure but about bouncing back stronger each time.

_Maintain an optimistic attitude_

You’ve probably heard this before, and for good reason—high-hope individuals try to find the positive side of everything that happens to them. They don’t allow the situation to dictate how they feel. They are not only optimistic but also creative problem solvers. They don’t give up easily when confronted with a challenge. Instead, they develop solutions that enable them to overcome the obstacles they face.

**But what if you feel hopeless?**

Yes, it’s normal for everyone to experience moments of hopelessness occasionally. Life brings challenges, setbacks, and unexpected changes that can make us feel overwhelmed, discouraged, or powerless. These feelings are often temporary, but they can still be challenging to manage when they arise.

For most people, hope fluctuates with their circumstances. A big disappointment, (https://hopetrustindia.com/blog/how-can-online-therapy-help-me-deal-with-post-breakup-heartache/), prolonged stress, or even minor challenges can temporarily diminish hope. However, these experiences often help you build resilience and learn to navigate difficulties, which can strengthen your sense of hope over time.

When hopelessness lingers or starts to affect everyday life, relationships, or your sense of purpose, it might signal something deeper, like depression or burnout, which can make it hard to see a way forward—in these cases, talking to friends or a counsellor.

**How does sharing feelings of hopelessness with a therapist help you?**

Sharing feelings of hopelessness with a therapist can help in several key ways:

_Unravelling the layers of hopelessness:_ Feelings of hopelessness are often intertwined with other emotions or stressors. A therapist’s guidance can help untangle these layers, leading to a deeper understanding of the root causes. This newfound clarity can be a significant relief and a crucial first step in managing these feelings.

_Breaking isolation:_ Hopelessness often makes people feel isolated, as if no one else could understand what they’re going through. Sharing these feelings with a compassionate therapist reduces isolation, allowing you to feel heard, validated, and understood.

_Equipping with coping strategies:_ Therapists can arm you with practical tools and coping strategies to manage hopelessness, whether through cognitive behavioural techniques, mindfulness practices, or other approaches. These tools are designed to empower you, allowing you to manage your thoughts and feelings.

_Building a sense of hope and direction:_ Therapists help clients find strengths, values, and past experiences that might feel buried under the weight of hopelessness. This process can help you rediscover a sense of hope and set realistic goals, which are essential in moving forward.

_Cultivating self-compassion:_ Therapists foster self-compassion and resilience, helping you become kinder to yourself and reframe negative thought patterns that can perpetuate hopelessness.
